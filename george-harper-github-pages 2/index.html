02

Stump Grinding

After a tree comes down, the stump can be a problem for years. We grind stumps below ground level so the area can be reused, replanted, or simply tidied up.

  • All stump sizes
  • Ground below surface level
  • Chippings removed or left
  • Area ready to replant
